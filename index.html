Everything in the bag

A full mini golf game made for the cabinet: the playfield, the DMD and the plunger all get used.

Plunger putting

Pull back the plunger to set your power and let go to putt. Prefer buttons? Use the power meter with A or the forward nudge.

1 to 4 players

Take turns on the same cabinet. Fewest strokes wins.

72 holes

Four courses of 18 holes each. Play the front 9, the back 9 or the full 18.

DMD celebrations

Hole in one, eagle, birdie and par each get their own animated show on the DMD screen.

Leaderboards

Each course keeps its own leaderboard with your initials, saved to the USB stick.

Pick an art style

Change the look of each course for something new on replay. Same bounces, different look.

Install in 4 steps

All you need is a USB stick and a Windows PC. It takes about two minutes.

1

Download the game

Click the gold button. Your browser saves the zip file to your Downloads folder.

⬇ Download free
2

Right-click the zip → Extract All…

Plug your USB stick into the PC. Open your Downloads folder, right-click MrLegendsMiniGolf-r1.zip and choose Extract All…

Choose your USB stick, then Extract

Click Browse…, pick your USB stick (for example USB Drive (E:)), click Select Folder, then Extract. The game lands in the right place on the stick by itself, inside a folder called external.

4

Eject, plug in, play

Safely eject the stick, plug it into your cabinet and choose Mr. Legends Mini Golf from the game list. Your leaderboards save to the stick, so leave it in while you play.

After you first play, you'll see 12 more folders on the stick named mini-golf-app-… (one for each course and length). That's where your scores are kept, so leave them there.

How to play

Everything works from the cabinet itself, no arcade control panel needed.

D-pad or flipper buttonsAim your putt
PlungerPull back to set power, release to putt
A button or forward nudgePutt with the power meter instead of the plunger
D-pad + AMove around the menus and select
BACKPause, or go back a screen
